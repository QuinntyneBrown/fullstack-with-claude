# Step 2 · Naming the project

**Model:** Opus 5.5. A good name is worth the stronger model.

**Prompt:** What would be a good unique name for a small notes app? Consider a particular meaning or association: biblical, abstract, descriptive.

**Candidates:**
- Selah: Hebrew, used in the Psalms, a pause to stop and reflect.
- Zikaron: Hebrew for memory or remembrance.
- Baruch: Jeremiah's scribe, who wrote down his words.
- Marginalia: notes written in a book's margins.

**Pick:** Selah. Short, easy to say, and the meaning fits a notes app. Repo: `QuinntyneBrown/selah`, public.
