Step 2 · The prompt

What would be a good unique name for a small notes app? Consider a particular meaning or association: biblical, abstract, descriptive.

The shape of the prompt

The product

A small notes app

The ask

A meaning or association, not a list of words

Three directions

Biblical, abstract, descriptive

Four candidates

Selah

Hebrew, used in the Psalms: a pause to stop and reflect

Zikaron

Hebrew for memory or remembrance

Baruch

Jeremiah's scribe, who wrote down his words

Marginalia

Notes written in a book's margins

The pick

Selah

  • Short
  • Easy to say
  • The meaning fits a notes app

The repository

QuinntyneBrown/selah

Public, because a reference app is meant to be read.

Things to remember

The shortest step, run on Opus: a good name is worth the stronger model
The prompt asked for a meaning or association: biblical, abstract, descriptive
Four candidates with meanings: Selah, Zikaron, Baruch, Marginalia
The pick is Selah: short, easy to say, a pause to reflect
